Dynamic Maze: Walls move, floors collapse, and new paths unlock over time to keep the maze constantly changing.
Smart Enemies: Enemies patrol, hear sounds, block paths, and intelligently chase the player instead of moving randomly.
Player Abilities System: Players use limited abilities like sprint, invisibility, slow-motion, and shields that can be upgraded.
Puzzle-Based Progression: Progress requires solving puzzles such as pressure plates, color doors, and lever-based mechanisms.
Risk–Reward System: Players choose between safer paths or dangerous routes that offer higher rewards and secret challenges.
